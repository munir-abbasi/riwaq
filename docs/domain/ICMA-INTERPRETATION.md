The interpretation phase is where observed graph and text patterns are evaluated as historical evidence. Software can organize the evidence and expose correlations, but historical conclusions remain explicit, reviewable inferences.

> **Scope:** This is methodological background. In Riwaq, family detection state, candidate outcome, and claim/evidence validity are separate axes and must not be collapsed into a single confidence or authenticity label.

**1. Evaluating Transmission Correlation**
When textual variations cluster with PCL sub-branches, the pattern can support a transmission-history hypothesis and make some alternative explanations less parsimonious. The correlation does not by itself prove that the transmission history is genuine or that every reported edge is historical.

* **Riwaq context:** Structural and textual features may contribute to candidate scoring under an explicit analysis profile. A high score is not export permission and should not be rendered as an automatic historical-authenticity verdict.

**2. Dating Recoverable Circulation**
ICMA does not prove that the Prophet Muhammad spoke the words. A Common Link plus correlated branch evidence may support an earliest recoverable circulation window within the analyzed corpus, subject to corpus completeness, identity resolution, chronology, and the independence of the surviving witnesses.

* **Riwaq context:** Biographical or geographical evidence can refine chronology only when it is actually supplied with provenance. An unresolved narrator reference is not a biography, and a structural CL candidate does not by itself fix an exact decade, city, or ultimate origin.

**3. Evaluating Bypass, Dive, and Spider Patterns**
A bypass or dive-like pattern occurs when a chain avoids an otherwise prominent Common Link structure. If its matn also shares wording strongly associated with a particular branch, that combination is a structural anomaly relevant to source criticism. It does not by itself establish that the chain was forged or identify who created it.

* **Riwaq context:** Software may detect and explain bypass-like structures and relevant textual similarities. Any stronger claim about fabrication must pass the appropriate evidence-binding and scholarly review rather than being inferred from the visual pattern alone.
